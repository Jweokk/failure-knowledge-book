    The Knowledge of Failure

    A CROSS-DOMAIN MANUAL OF STRUCTURAL FAILURE

    Twenty-four mechanisms, fourteen domains, 159 cases. Each chapter takes one way things break: how it grows, what shows first, and the conditions under which it cannot be stopped. Every case stops at the decision point; the outcome waits past the page.

    Four ways to use it

    LOOK UP Arrive with a situation: the situating table in Chapter 35 gives the direction, then the mechanism-by-domain matrix and the case index.

    USE You have a meeting: thirty minutes before the decision, a design review question list, a ninety-minute failure rehearsal.

    PRACTISE Each card stops at the decision point with three questions. Write your answer before you turn the page.

    SHARE Explaining failure to a team: how to write a postmortem still useful in three years, and the documents six mature fields actually keep.
