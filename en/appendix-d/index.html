    Appendix D: A Genealogy of Mechanisms

    The twenty-four mechanisms are not independent entries standing side by side. They are twenty-four projections on the surface of a few tensions underneath. This appendix records the family relations among them: which ones usually travel in pairs, which one disguises itself as which, and what a combination means when it shows up. It cross-links with Section 6 of each mechanism chapter.

    D.1 Three underlying tensions

    Tension one: knowing against admitting, in the information layer. The organization knows the truth, or could know it, but the cost of admitting it deforms the truth in the archive, in the reporting and in the narrative. Family members: Normalization of Deviance (11, distortion at the archival layer), Narrative before Evidence (13, distortion at the expressive layer), Information Attenuation up the Hierarchy (16, distortion at the transmission layer), Why the Truth-Teller Goes First (19, silencing at the source), Goal Displacement; Goodhart's Law (12, distortion at the measurement layer). All five share one physical process: the truth is distorted layer by layer inside the organization's information structure. Reading a combination: when more than three of the five appear together, the organization's sensing system has failed as a whole, no single-point repair will help, and an independent sensing channel has to be brought in from outside (the devices of Chapters 16 and 19).

    Tension two: now against later, in the time layer. Present gain is traded for future cost, and the structure permits it. Family members: Horizon Mismatch (28, the tenure structure), The Invisibility of Maintenance (29, the asset side), Margin Erosion (25, the design side), The Slack of Success (20, the will side). All four run on one licence: someone later pays. Reading a combination: in an organization where all four appear, the risk state is older than any indicator shows, since indicators reflect the current period while the debt sits buried on the time axis (Surfside is the specimen holding all four: ce-009).

    Tension three: one against all, in the structural layer. Between local rationality and collective loss sits an externality that cannot be priced. Family members: Misaligned Incentives (17, the direction of interest), Diffusion of Responsibility (18, where responsibility is lodged), Cascading Failure (33, transmission through dependency), Crossing a Scale Threshold (24, the limit of coordination). Reading a combination: in an organization running both 17 and 18, wrong direction with nobody accountable, failure is only a matter of time; in an industry running both 33 and 24, dependency concentrated and scale expanding, what is being waited for is simultaneous failure (the 38 shell consultants of Chapter 24, the thousand cloud customers of Chapter 33).

    D.3 Mutual disguise, and how to tell them apart

    Disguise one: 17 dressed as 12. An appraisal problem gets discussed in the shape of an indicator design problem: change the indicator, leave the incentive alone. The test: does behaviour go back to the old pattern once the indicator changes? If it goes back, 17 is running (Chapter 12, Section 6).

    Disguise two: 25 dressed as 29. Eating the margin, which is cutting redundancy, and arrears in maintenance, which is deferring service, get lumped together as aging. The test: is the object a design value (25) or a condition value (29)? At Surfside both layers were present.

    Disguise three: 19 dressed as 16. When no news comes up the channel, separate first whether nobody spoke (19) or somebody spoke and it was diluted (16). The diagnostic question: the last time someone spoke sharply, what became of that person?

    Disguise four: 24 dressed as 22. Simultaneous failure exposed by growth gets handled as common-cause failure, and the answer is another backup. The test: is the trigger external (22) or the scale itself (24)? GitHub's record load (24) against the Arkema flood (22) is the textbook contrast.

    Disguise five: 30 dressed as 18. Nobody being accountable after a broken handover looks like diffusion, when the knowledge never arrived at all. The test: does the receiving side know it is accountable? If not, 30. If it knows and assumes someone else will handle it, 18.

    D.4 Combination warnings

    Three combinations, each of which on appearing calls for handling to be escalated at once, from a ledger walkthrough to a dedicated review.

    Combination one, the sensing-failure package: three of 11, 13, 16 and 19 present. The organization is already systematically deaf, and the sensing gets repaired before anything else is discussed.

    Combination two, the time-bomb package: two of 25, 28 and 29 present. The debt sits on the time axis and has no signatory, which is the reading of Surfside and of Westminster.

    Combination three, the systemic-exposure package: two of 21, 24 and 33 present. The shared platform of an expansion phase is accumulating the conditions for simultaneous failure, and sw-005 in Chapter 24 and sw-003 in Chapter 33 come from the same company, which is not a coincidence.

    Three notes on use. The genealogy is a map for reading, not a pedigree for theory: two chapters sitting next to each other does not mean two mechanisms share an origin, only that their tests are easily confused, and the boundary discussion in Section 6 is the formal text that separates them. The three-generation division, upstream disciplinary concept, mechanism chapter, field form, matches three citation needs: cite upstream in a paper, use the mechanism chapter in management, copy the field form into a ledger, and each reader takes the layer they need. The genealogy grows, since Section 6's boundary discussions are revised with each version, so the table gets checked against the body text once a year and new adjacencies are added as they appear; the table is built as a living document.
