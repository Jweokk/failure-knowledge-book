# Appendix E: Mechanism Genealogy

> The twenty-four mechanisms are not parallel independent items; they are twenty-four surface projections of a small number of underlying tensions. This appendix records the family relationships: which mechanisms co-occur, who impersonates whom, and what it means when combinations appear. Cross-linked with each mechanism chapter's Section 6.

## E.1 Three Underlying Tensions

Tension one: knowing vs acknowledging (information layer). The organisation knows or could know the truth, but the cost of acknowledgment deforms it in archives, reports, and narratives. Family members: Normalisation of Deviance (11, archive-layer deformation), Narrative before Evidence (13, expression-layer deformation), Information Attenuation (16, transmission-layer deformation), Whistleblower Suppression (19, source-layer silencing), Goal Displacement (12, measurement-layer deformation). All five share one physical process: truth deforms layer by layer through the organisation's information structure. Combination reading: when three or more of the five co-occur, the organisation's perceptual system has failed wholesale; no single-point fix will work — an independent sensing channel must be introduced from outside.

Tension two: now vs later (time layer). Present benefit and future cost are traded continuously under structural permission. Family members: Horizon Mismatch (28, tenure structure), Invisible Maintenance (29, asset side), Margin Erosion (25, design side), Failure of Success (20, willingness side). All four share the licence "someone else pays later." Combination reading: an organisation showing three or more of these four is older than any of its indicators suggest — the debt is buried on the timeline (Surfside is a specimen of all four combined: ce-009).

Tension three: one vs all (structure layer). Local rationality and collective loss are separated by externalities that cannot be priced within any single layer's ledger. Family members: Misaligned Incentives (17, interest direction), Diffusion of Responsibility (18, accountability locus), Cascading Failure (33, dependency propagation), Nonlinear Scaling (24, coordination limits). Combination reading: 17＋18 co-occurring (wrong direction AND no one responsible) means failure is a matter of time; 33＋24 co-occurring (concentrated dependencies AND rapid scaling) means the industry is waiting for a synchronous event.

## E.3 Mutual Disguise and How to Tell Them Apart

Disguise one: 17 impersonating 12. Incentive problems frequently present as "metric design problems" (change the metric, leave the incentive alone). Test: after changing the metric, does behaviour revert? If yes, 17 is running.

Disguise two: 25 impersonating 29. Eating margin (cutting redundancy) and deferring maintenance (skipping upkeep) are both called "ageing." Test: is the object a design value (25) or a condition value (29)? Surfside has both layers.

Disguise three: 19 impersonating 16. When the channel carries no messages, first distinguish: is no one speaking (19), or are they speaking and being diluted (16)? Criterion question: what happened to the last person who spoke sharply?

Disguise four: 24 impersonating 22. Scale-exposed "simultaneous failures" are often misfiled as common-cause failures (response: add backups). Test: was the trigger an external cause (22) or scale itself (24)? GitHub's record-load day (24) versus Arkema's flood (22) is the textbook contrast.

Disguise five: 30 impersonating 18. Post-handover orphans look like dilution but are really knowledge that never arrived. Criterion: does the successor know they are responsible? Not knowing = 30; knowing but assuming someone else will act = 18.

## E.4 Combination Alerts

Three combinations that warrant immediate escalation from ledger review to special treatment:

Combination one (perceptual failure package): three or more of {11, 13, 16, 19} — the organisation is systemically deaf. Fix perception before anything else.

Combination two (time bomb package): two or more of {25, 28, 29} — debt is on the timeline with no signatory. Read Surfside and Westminster this way.

Combination three (systemic exposure package): two or more of {21, 24, 33} — a shared substrate under expansion is accumulating the conditions for synchronous failure. Chapters 24 (sw-005) and 33 (sw-003) come from the same company; that is not a coincidence.
