How to read it. By row: each row shows a mechanism's evidential footprint across domains; the widest rows (Hidden Coupling, Invisible Maintenance, Practical Drift) are the most domain-portable. By column: each column shows a domain's mechanism density; the emptiest columns are either record skew at work (Ch. 2) or a sampling gap of this book — either way, the right response is to build a local sample. At the blanks: a blank never means "this domain has no such mechanism"; it means this book holds no verified primary text that evidences one. Appendix D's interpretation rules outrank this table.
