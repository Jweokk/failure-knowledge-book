# The Knowledge of Failure

<p class="sub">A CROSS-DOMAIN MANUAL OF STRUCTURAL FAILURE</p>

<p class="lead">Twenty-four mechanisms, fourteen domains, 159 cases. Each chapter takes one way things break: how it grows, what shows first, and the conditions under which it cannot be stopped. Every case stops at the decision point; the outcome waits past the page.</p>
